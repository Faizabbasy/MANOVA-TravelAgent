## Roadmap 25-Tahap (Section 00–24) — SELESAI

**Section 24 — Full Regression dan Final Implementation Guide, COMPLETED 2026-08-01 — ini adalah section TERAKHIR dari roadmap 25-tahap (Section 00–24).** Seluruh Section 01–23 sudah COMPLETED lebih dulu (lihat tabel di atas + `docs/mockup-section-reports/`); Section 24 menutup roadmap dengan: (1) regresi penuh — seluruh 89 route/82 page file diaudit HTTP+konten, 16 role diaudit akses/aksi inti, rantai workflow utama Lead→Qualification→AE→Opportunity→Quotation→Management Approval→Client Confirmation→Won→Active Client→Project Order→Handover→Planning→Traveler→Supplier→Booking→Ready→On Trip→Changes/Incident→Finance→Completed→Closed (23 langkah) diverifikasi lengkap dengan sitasi file/fungsi nyata, plus 9 edge case (rejection/revision/duplicate-prevention/cancellation/refund/incident/overdue/supplier-failure/schedule-change); (2) 2 bug nyata ditemukan dan diperbaiki (`sourceBreakdown` mutating cached computed di `lead-sources/index.vue`; variable-shadowing `traveler` di Accommodation/Transportation detail); (3) tooling lint (`eslint`)/typecheck (`vue-tsc`) terpasang dan diverifikasi (Q8 RESOLVED — lihat `docs/mockup-open-questions.md`); (4) mekanisme **Project Closed** dibangun nyata untuk pertama kalinya (`evaluateProjectClosureGate`/`closeProject`/`getProjectClosureSummary`, mengisi `ProjectClosureChecklist` shell sejak Section 09/D-066, pola sama Financial Closure Gate Section 20/D-077); (5) seluruh dokumentasi final ditulis — `docs/frontend-end-to-end-implementation-guide.md` dan `docs/frontend-demo-and-review-guide.md` (baru), plus final pass `docs/frontend-module-map.md`/`docs/frontend-workflow-map.md`/`docs/frontend-implementation-roadmap.md` (dokumen ini)/`docs/frontend-known-issues.md`/`docs/mockup-implementation-state.md`/`docs/mockup-final-route-inventory-v2.md`.
